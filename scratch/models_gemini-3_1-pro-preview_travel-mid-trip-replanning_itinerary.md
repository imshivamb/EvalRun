### **Revised Itinerary: Days 13–28**

#### **Day 13: Typhoon Day in Hiroshima (Stay Indoors)**
*   **Logistics:** The ferries are canceled, so stay safe in the city center. Avoid coastal areas.
*   **Activities:** Head to the **Hondori Shopping Arcade** (fully covered). This is perfect for your thrifting interests. Visit **Kinji Used Clothing** and **2nd Street** for vintage finds. 
*   **Food:** Eat at **Okonomimura** (Okonomiyaki Village)—a multi-floor indoor food hub featuring cheap, filling Hiroshima-style savory pancakes (~¥900). 
*   **Photography:** Rainy cyberpunk/neon street photography inside the covered arcades and looking out into the wet streets.

#### **Day 14: Miyajima & Transit to Kyoto**
*   **Morning (Miyajima):** The typhoon should have passed. Take the early JR train to Miyajimaguchi and the ferry to Miyajima Island (¥500 round trip). Photograph the Floating Torii Gate and the wild deer. Leave the island by 12:00 PM.
*   **Afternoon Transit (Budget Hack):** Skip Himeji Castle to save time and money. Instead of the Shinkansen (¥11,500), book a daytime highway bus (e.g., Willer Express or JR Bus) from Hiroshima Station to Kyoto Station. It takes about 6 hours but costs only **~¥4,500**, saving you ~¥7,000. 
*   **Evening:** Arrive in Kyoto. Check into your fixed-date budget guesthouse. Grab a cheap dinner at a *Konbini* (7-Eleven/Lawson).

#### **Day 15: Kyoto – Higashiyama (Vintage & Heritage)**
*   **Morning:** Walk through Yasaka Shrine to **Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka** early in the morning for crowd-free photography of the traditional wooden streets. 
*   **Afternoon:** Walk down to the **Teramachi and Shinkyogoku Covered Shopping Arcades**. Visit **Harajuku Chicago** (excellent for cheap, vintage kimonos and streetwear) and **Flamingo**.
*   **Coffee:** Stop by a traditional *Kissaten* (retro Japanese coffee shop) for a cheap "Morning Set" (coffee and thick toast for ~¥500).

#### **Day 16: Kyoto – Fushimi Inari & South Kyoto**
*   **Morning:** Wake up very early (arrive by 6:30 AM) to hike **Fushimi Inari Shrine**. It is completely free, and getting there early ensures stunning photography without the crowds.
*   **Afternoon:** Take the train back to the city center. Walk along the **Kamo River** (free and beautiful) and grab a budget-friendly lunch at a local chain like Matsuya or Nakau (~¥600 for a beef bowl).

#### **Day 17: Kyoto – Arashiyama**
*   **Morning:** Head west to the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (Free). 
*   **Afternoon:** Photograph the Togetsukyo Bridge. Visit **% Arabica Kyoto Arashiyama** for great coffee with a riverside view. To save money, skip the expensive paid temples in the area and explore the free walking trails along the Katsura River. 

#### **Day 18: Kyoto – Philosopher's Path & Relax**
*   **Morning:** Walk the **Philosopher’s Path** (Free). It’s a peaceful canal walk lined with local cafes and small artisan shops. 
*   **Afternoon:** Your final day to enjoy your prepaid guesthouse. Visit a local supermarket around 7:00 PM to buy discounted Bento boxes for dinner (often marked down by 30-50%).

#### **Day 19: Kyoto to Tokyo (Major Budget Swap)**
*   **Transit:** Instead of the Shinkansen (¥14,500), take a daytime **Willer Express Highway Bus** from Kyoto to Tokyo (takes ~7.5 hours, costs **~¥4,500**). You can enjoy the scenic countryside view, rest, and edit your photos. 
*   **Evening:** Arrive in Tokyo (usually Shinjuku or Tokyo Station). Check into your Tokyo hostel.

#### **Day 20: Tokyo – Shimokitazawa (Thrift Capital)**
*   **Activities:** Spend the day in Shimokitazawa, Tokyo's vintage heaven. It is incredibly photogenic. 
*   **Thrifting:** Visit **Stick Out** (everything is around ¥700), **New York Joe Exchange** (housed in an old bathhouse), and **Treasure Factory Style**. 
*   **Vibe:** Lots of cheap indie cafes and great street-style photography opportunities.

#### **Day 21: Tokyo – Koenji (Cheaper Vintage)**
*   *Note: teamLab is closed today.*
*   **Activities:** Head to Koenji, which is slightly grittier and cheaper than Shimokitazawa. 
*   **Thrifting:** Visit **Mode Off** for massive discounts on used clothing, and **Sokkyo**. Koenji is famous for its underground music scene, making it perfect for alternative photography. 

#### **Day 22: Tokyo – Shinjuku (Free Views & Cyberpunk Photos)**
*   *Note: Original teamLab day (Closed).*
*   **Morning:** Visit the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** in Shinjuku. The observation deck on the 45th floor is completely **free** and offers panoramic views of Tokyo.
*   **Afternoon/Evening:** Photograph the neon lights of **Kabukicho** and the narrow, smoky alleyways of **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane). 

#### **Day 23: Tokyo – Harajuku & Shibuya**
*   *Note: teamLab is closed today.*
*   **Morning:** Walk through the massive **Yoyogi Park** and **Meiji Shrine** (Free). 
*   **Afternoon:** Walk down Omotesando and dive into **Ura-Harajuku** (the backstreets). This is prime territory for street-fashion photography and small indie coffee stands. 
*   **Evening:** Photograph the **Shibuya Crossing** at sunset from the Mag's Park rooftop or the station windows.

#### **Day 24: Tokyo – teamLab Planets & Odaiba**
*   **Morning:** **teamLab Planets** (Toyosu). *Ensure you have rescheduled your ticket to this day!* 
*   **Afternoon:** Take the Yurikamome Line (great front-window photo ops of the city skyline) to Odaiba. Photograph the life-sized Gundam statue and Tokyo Bay.

#### **Day 25: Tokyo – Akihabara & Ueno**
*   **Morning:** Walk through **Ueno Park** (Free).
*   **Afternoon:** Shop at **Ameya-Yokocho (Ameyoko)**, a bustling open-air market under the train tracks. Excellent for cheap street food, discounted shoes, and vibrant street photography.
*   **Evening:** Head to **Akihabara** for incredible night photography of the anime/electronics district.

#### **Day 26: Tokyo – Asakusa**
*   **Morning:** Visit **Senso-ji Temple**. Go before 9:00 AM to photograph the temple and the Nakamise shopping street without the massive crowds.
*   **Afternoon:** Walk along the Sumida River for photos of the Tokyo Skytree.

#### **Day 27: Tokyo – Final Exploration**
*   **Activities:** Keep this day open. Revisit any neighborhood you loved, or explore Nakameguro for aesthetic cafes. 
*   **Dinner:** Treat yourself (budget-friendly) at a conveyor belt sushi chain like **Kura Sushi** or **Sushiro** (~¥150 per plate) for your final night.

#### **Day 28: Departure (Locked End)**
*   **Transit to Airport:** Do not take the expensive Narita Express (¥3,200) or Keisei Skyliner (¥2,500). Instead, take the **Airport Bus TYO-NRT** from Tokyo Station or Ginza. It costs exactly **¥1,300**, takes 65 minutes, and drops you right at your terminal.
