### 1. Work Hours & Time Zone Conversion

South Korea Standard Time (KST) and Japan Standard Time (JST) both operate at **UTC+9:00**, which is **3 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30)**.

| Window | Indian Standard Time (IST) | Korea & Japan Time (KST / JST) | Required Action / Activity Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Morning Free Block** | Pre-12:30 PM | **Early Morning – 4:00 PM** | Major sightseeing, hikes, photo walks, long-distance intercity transit |
| **Workspace Arrival** | 12:45 PM | **4:15 PM (Firm)** | Settle in quiet hostel desk/cafe with verified high-speed Wi-Fi |
| **Critical Meeting Block** | **1:00 PM – 2:00 PM** | **4:30 PM – 5:30 PM (Firm)** | **Stationary video calls only** (no walking, transit, or noisy areas) |
| **Core Availability Window** | **12:30 PM – 8:30 PM** | **4:00 PM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)** | **Async Roaming / Flexible Work** (cafes, night markets, Shinkansen, hostel lounge) |

### Backpacking & Remote Work Essentials

1. **Connectivity**: 
   * Reserve an **Unlimited e-SIM (Ubigi or Airalo)** for your smartphone hotspot backup.
   * Hostels recommended (*CITAN*, *Nui.*, *Piece Hostel*, *K's House*) all feature designated co-working tables, private phone/meeting booths, and fiber-optic Wi-Fi (>100 Mbps).
2. **Transit Passes**:
   * **South Korea**: Purchase a T-Money card at Incheon; book KTX via Korail app 3 weeks in advance.
   * **Japan**: Load a digital Suica/Pasmo card on your phone for urban subways; purchase individual Shinkansen tickets via SmartEX app for flexibility.
3. **Power & Quiet Spaces**:
   * In Japan, if caught outdoors near the 16:15 deadline, look for **"Coin Space"** (drop-in hourly coworking spaces found near major train stations) or private **Telecube / Station Work** soundproof booths inside JR stations.
