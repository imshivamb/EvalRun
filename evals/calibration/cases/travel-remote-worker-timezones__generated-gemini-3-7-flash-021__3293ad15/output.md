# 28-Day Remote Work & Backpacking Itinerary: South Korea & Japan (October)

---

## 1. Remote Work & Time Zone Conversion Architecture

* **Indian Standard Time (IST)**: UTC +5:30  
* **Korea Standard Time (KST) / Japan Standard Time (JST)**: UTC +9:00  
* **Time Difference**: KST and JST are **+3 hours 30 minutes ahead** of IST.

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| IST (Home Base)  | 12:30 PM                           1:00 PM - 2:00 PM                   8:30 PM  |
| KST / JST (Trip) |  4:00 PM (Shift Begins)            4:30 PM - 5:30 PM (Fixed Meeting)  12:00 AM  |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Activity Profile | [Free Morning / Travel] -> [Settled by 4:15 PM] -> [Async / Roam Work]          |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Daily Weekday Framework (Monday – Friday)
* **07:30 – 15:30 KST/JST (Morning Exploration & Travel Window)**: 8 uninterrupted hours for sightseeing, photography, day-tripping, walking tours, and transit.
* **15:30 – 16:15 KST/JST (Transition & Buffer)**: Return to hostel workspace or arrive at a verified, work-friendly café. Run a network speed test.
* **16:15 – 17:30 KST/JST (Fixed Meeting Block — 1:00–2:00 PM IST)**: Stationary video-call environment with verified Wi-Fi and quiet acoustics.
* **17:30 – 00:00 KST/JST (Flexible Async Work & Roam Window — 2:00–8:30 PM IST)**: Complete 3–5 hours of asynchronous tasks on-the-go from night markets, laptop-friendly kissaten/cafés, or hostel common areas.
* **Weekends (Saturday & Sunday)**: 100% work-free for long-range transit, alpine day trips, and deep immersion.

## 2. Practical Remote Backpacking Cheat-Sheet

| Requirement | Strategy & Execution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SIM / Backup Data** | Carry an unlimited **eSIM** (Ubigi / Airalo on NTT Docomo or SoftBank networks) + phone tethering as hot failover for the 16:15–17:30 JST meeting window. |
| **Power Infrastructure** | Hostels and cafés in Japan use Type A/B ungrounded plugs (100V). Bring a flat 2-prong power bank (65W GaN PD charger recommended for fast laptop top-ups). |
| **Quiet Meeting Spaces** | If outside the hostel during the 16:15 JST buffer, look for **Telecube / Station Work** soundproof booths at JR stations (approx. ¥275 / 15 mins, reserve via smartphone web app). |
| **Luggage Logistics** | Utilize **Takkyubin (Yamato Transport)** luggage forwarding between major hubs (e.g., Osaka to Tokyo) for ~¥2,000 per bag, freeing you to roam on travel days. |
