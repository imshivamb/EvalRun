### 1. Time Zone & Remote Work Framework

#### Time Zone Conversions
* **Indian Standard Time (IST)**: UTC +5:30
* **Korea Standard Time (KST) / Japan Standard Time (JST)**: UTC +9:00
* **Time Difference**: KST and JST are **3 hours and 30 minutes ahead** of IST.

| Work Obligation | India Time (IST) | Local Time (KST / JST) | Required Environment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-Meeting Setup** | 12:45 PM | **4:15 PM** | Settled at work base (Hostel desk / Quiet Wi-Fi café) |
| **Critical Meeting Block** | 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM | **4:30 PM – 5:30 PM** | **Fixed & Quiet** (Dedicated Wi-Fi, zero transit) |
| **Flexible Async Window** | 2:00 PM – 8:30 PM | **5:30 PM – Midnight** | **Mobile / Anywhere** (Cafés, night markets, trains) |
| **Exploration / Sightseeing** | Free Hours | **6:00 AM – 3:45 PM** | Sightseeing, photography, hiking, transit |

---

### Standard Weekday Blueprint (Monday – Friday)

* **07:30 – 15:30 | Exploration & Major Sightseeing**: Temples, photography walks, vintage shopping, transit between cities, and lunch.
* **15:30 – 16:15 | Return to Base**: Transition to hostel common area/room or a verified co-working/quiet café.
* **16:15 – 17:30 | 🔒 FIXED MEETING WINDOW**: Stationary, reliable Wi-Fi, noise-dampened space for video calls.
* **17:30 – 24:00 | 📱 FLEXIBLE WORK & EVENING IMMERSION**: Roaming street food alleys, thrifting, evening photography, working asynchronously from laptop-friendly cafés and dining spots.

*Weekends (Saturdays & Sundays) are 100% work-free for full-day excursions and nature hikes.*

## 28-Day Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Week 1: South Korea (Seoul & Busan)

#### Day 1 (Mon) – Arrival in Seoul & Hongdae Vibes
* **Base**: Hostel in Hongdae (e.g., *Zzzip Guesthouse* or *Time Travelers Party Hostel*).
* **Morning/Early Afternoon**: Land at Incheon Airport (ICN), take the AREX train to Hongdae, check in, and explore the street art and indie shops of Yeonnam-dong.
* **16:15 – 17:30 [Fixed Meeting]**: Hostel workspace / quiet café (*Café Yan* or *Coffee Brown*).
* **17:30 – 24:00 [Async Work & Roam]**: Hongdae night market, busking performances, Korean fried chicken dinner while clearing async tasks.

#### Day 2 (Tue) – Historic Seoul & Hipjiro Alleyways
* **Morning/Early Afternoon**: Early photography at Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon Hanok Village. Walk through Insadong traditional tea street.
* **15:30 – 16:15**: Head to Euljiro ("Hipjiro") co-working cafe or back to hostel.
* **16:15 – 17:30 [Fixed Meeting]**: *Coffee Hanyakbang* (private corner) or hostel.
* **17:30 – 24:00 [Async Work & Roam]**: Euljiro neon back-alleys (retro industrial vibes, pojangmacha street food carts, casual laptop check-ins).

#### Day 3 (Wed) – Seongsu-dong: The Brooklyn of Seoul
* **Morning/Early Afternoon**: Walk through Seoul Forest; explore repurposed warehouse boutiques and vintage pop-ups along Seongsu-dong.
* **15:30 – 16:15**: Settle into *Daelim Changgo* or *Center Coffee*.
* **16:15 – 17:30 [Fixed Meeting]**: High-speed Wi-Fi café work block in Seongsu.
* **17:30 – 24:00 [Async Work & Roam]**: Ttukseom Hangang Park for sunset photography, Han River ramen, night skyline.

#### Day 4 (Thu) – Vintage Thrifting Deep-Dive: Dongmyo & Ikseon-dong
* **Morning/Early Afternoon**: Dongmyo Flea Market (Seoul’s premier vintage clothing street market). Continue to Ikseon-dong hanok alleyways.
* **15:30 – 16:15**: Return to base in Hongdae / Jongno café.
* **16:15 – 17:30 [Fixed Meeting]**: Fixed base call.
* **17:30 – 24:00 [Async Work & Roam]**: Gwangjang Market (Mungbean pancakes, Mayak gimbap) with mobile email monitoring.

#### Day 5 (Fri) – High-Speed Transit to Busan
* **Morning (09:00 – 12:30)**: KTX Bullet Train from Seoul Station to Busan (2.5 hrs). Check into hostel near Nampo-dong or Seomyeon (*Kimchee Busan Station* or *One Way Guesthouse*).
* **13:00 – 15:30**: Jagalchi Fish Market and BIFF Square street food.
* **16:15 – 17:30 [Fixed Meeting]**: Hostel dedicated workstation.
* **17:30 – 24:00 [Async Work & Roam]**: Gwangalli Beach evening walk, Diamond Bridge light show, coastal café working.

#### Day 6 (Sat - Weekend) – Busan Coastal Hikes & Culture
* **All Day (Work-Free)**: Gamcheon Culture Village early morning for photography; Huinnyeoul Culture Village cliffside walk; sunset at Songdo Cloud Trails.

#### Day 7 (Sun - Weekend) – Coastal Trail & Flight to Fukuoka (Japan)
* **All Day (Work-Free)**: Igidae Coastal Walk (Oryukdo skywalk).
* **Evening**: 45-minute flight from Busan (PUS) to Fukuoka (FUK) or overnight Camellia Line ferry. Check into Fukuoka hostel (e.g., *WeBase Hakata*).

### Key Backpacking & Work Tips

1. **Hostel Selection**: Properties like *Nui. Hostel* (Tokyo), *Len* (Kyoto), and *WeBase* (Fukuoka) have dedicated café/co-working lounges with high-speed fiber Wi-Fi and power outlets at tables.
2. **Connectivity Backup**: Rent an unlimited **eSIM or Pocket Wi-Fi** (e.g., Ubigi, Ninja WiFi, or KT Olleh) to ensure video calls never drop if switching away from hostel networks.
3. **Luggage Forwarding (*Takkyubin*)**: Use budget luggage forwarding or train station coin lockers when transitioning between mountain cities (Takayama/Matsumoto) so you can roam unencumbered.
