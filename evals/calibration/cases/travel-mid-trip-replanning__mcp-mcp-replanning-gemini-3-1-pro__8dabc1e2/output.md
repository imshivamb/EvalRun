### 💡 Budget Recovery Strategy (Target: Save ₹20,000 / ~35,000 JPY)
To offset your emergency expense without ruining your trip, we will make the following swaps:
1. **Transport Swaps (Save ~15,000 JPY):** Swap the Shinkansen (bullet trains) for Highway Buses (e.g., Willer Express) for the Hiroshima $\rightarrow$ Kyoto and Kyoto $\rightarrow$ Tokyo legs. Also, use the local Keisei line to Narita instead of the expensive Narita Express.
2. **Food & Dining (Save ~15,000 JPY):** Swap one restaurant meal a day for a high-quality *konbini* (convenience store) meal or supermarket discount bento. 
3. **Activity Swaps (Save ~5,000 JPY):** Skip the paid entry to Himeji Castle (which is complicated by the bus route anyway) and the canceled Miyajima ferry, focusing instead on free street photography and vintage window-shopping.

### 🗺️ Revised Itinerary (Days 13–28)

**Day 13: Hiroshima (Typhoon Reroute)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** With the Miyajima ferry canceled and heavy rain expected, stay indoors. Head to the **Hondori Covered Shopping Arcade**. It is completely shielded from the weather and features several local thrift stores (like *Kinji*) and cozy cafes where you can wait out the storm.
*   **Evening:** For dinner, visit **Okonomimura** (Okonomiyaki Village), a multi-floor indoor food hub to try Hiroshima-style savory pancakes. It’s cheap, filling, and perfect for a rainy night.

**Day 14: Hiroshima to Kyoto (Budget Travel Day)**
*   **Morning:** Check out of your Hiroshima hostel. Instead of taking the expensive Shinkansen via Himeji, take a daytime **Highway Bus** directly to Kyoto (takes about 6-7 hours, but saves you roughly 6,000 JPY). 
*   **Afternoon/Evening:** Arrive in Kyoto. Check into your pre-paid, non-refundable guesthouse. 
*   **Photography:** Walk around the Kamo River at golden hour—a great, free spot for photography.

**Day 15: Kyoto (Fushimi Inari & Thrifting)**
*   **Morning:** Head to **Fushimi Inari Shrine** as early as possible (6:00 AM - 7:00 AM) to beat the crowds and get moody, atmospheric photographs of the torii gates (100% free).
*   **Afternoon:** Explore the **Teramachi Kyogoku** shopping arcade. It’s a great area for vintage clothing (check out *Chicago* and *Flamingo*) and budget-friendly cafes. 

**Day 16: Kyoto (Arashiyama Bamboo Grove)**
*   **Morning:** Visit the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** early (free). Rather than paying to enter the surrounding temples, hike up to Kameyama-koen Park at the end of the grove for a free, stunning view of the Katsura River.
*   **Afternoon:** Grab a coffee at the famous % Arabica Kyoto Arashiyama (budget a small treat here for the view) and take photos around the Togetsukyo Bridge.

**Day 17: Kyoto (Higashiyama Street Photography)**
*   **All Day:** Spend the day doing a walking photography tour of the **Higashiyama District**. Walk from Kiyomizu-dera (viewing the outside is free) down through Sannen-zaka and Ninen-zaka streets, capturing the traditional wooden architecture. 
*   **Budget Tip:** Grab lunch from a FamilyMart or 7-Eleven and eat it at Maruyama Park.

**Day 18: Kyoto (Philosopher’s Path)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Walk the **Philosopher's Path** (Tetsugaku-no-michi). It’s a beautiful, peaceful walk along a canal lined with small, independent cafes. 
*   **Evening:** Pack and prepare for your bus ride to Tokyo tomorrow.

**Day 19: Kyoto to Tokyo (Budget Travel Day)**
*   **Morning:** Check out of your Kyoto guesthouse. Take a daytime Highway Bus from Kyoto to Tokyo (e.g., Willer Express). This will take about 7–8 hours but will save you around 8,000–9,000 JPY compared to the Shinkansen.
*   **Evening:** Arrive in Tokyo, check into your Tokyo hostel, and grab a cheap bowl of ramen nearby.

**Day 20: Tokyo (Shimokitazawa Vintage District)**
*   **All Day:** Dive into **Shimokitazawa**. This is Tokyo’s premier neighborhood for vintage thrifting, indie music, and quirky cafes. Even if you don't buy much due to the budget cut, the window shopping and street fashion photography here are top-tier. 

**Day 21: Tokyo (Shinjuku & Harajuku)** *[teamLab is Closed]*
*   **Morning:** Visit **Meiji Shrine** and the adjacent Yoyogi Park (both free). Walk down Takeshita Street in Harajuku for vibrant, chaotic street photography.
*   **Evening:** Head to **Shinjuku**. Photograph the neon lights of Kabukicho and Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane). You don't need to eat at the expensive izakayas here; just capturing the atmosphere is incredible. 

**Day 22: Tokyo (Shibuya & Street Culture)** *[Original teamLab Day]*
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Walk across the famous **Shibuya Crossing**. Head up to the Miyashita Park rooftop (free entry) to photograph the skate park and urban architecture. 
*   **Evening:** Explore Shibuya's side streets for hidden cafes and record stores.

**Day 23: Tokyo (Koenji Thrifting)** *[teamLab is Closed]*
*   **All Day:** Head to **Koenji**. This neighborhood is slightly more underground and often cheaper than Shimokitazawa. It is packed with vintage stores (like *Sokkyo* and *Don Don Down on Wednesday*, which has massive discounts) and cheap street food. 

**Day 24: Tokyo (Rescheduled teamLab Planets)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** **teamLab Planets** (Toyosu). *Conditional Action: You must log into your teamLab account online as soon as possible to modify your ticket date from Day 22 to Day 24. They allow date changes subject to availability.*
*   **Evening:** Walk around Odaiba or Toyosu for futuristic architecture and waterfront photography.

**Day 25: Tokyo (Asakusa & Ueno - Budget Shopping)**
*   **Morning:** Visit **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa (free). 
*   **Afternoon:** Walk to Ueno Park, then explore **Ameyoko Street**. Ameyoko is an open-air market where you can find some of the cheapest street food, snacks, and discounted clothes in Tokyo.

**Day 26: Tokyo (Architecture & Window Shopping)**
*   **Morning:** Visit **Ginza** for sleek, modern architecture photography. 
*   **Afternoon:** Walk to **Akihabara** for retro gaming and anime culture. Even without spending money, the sensory overload makes for incredible photos.

**Day 27: Tokyo (Local Neighborhoods)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Spend your final full day wandering through quieter, aesthetic neighborhoods like **Nakameguro** or **Daikanyama**. These areas are famous for high-end boutique cafes, but grabbing a takeaway coffee and strolling along the Meguro River is highly budget-friendly and great for photography.
*   **Evening:** Farewell dinner—treat yourself to an affordable conveyer-belt sushi meal (like *Kura Sushi* or *Sushiro*).

**Day 28: Tokyo Departure (Narita Airport)**
*   **Morning:** Check out of your hostel.
*   **Transit to NRT:** To stick to your budget, bypass the expensive Keisei Skyliner or Narita Express (N'EX). Instead, take the **Keisei Main Line Limited Express** or the **Airport Bus TYO-NRT** (both cost roughly 1,050 to 1,300 JPY), saving you a final 1,500+ JPY.
*   Depart for home!
