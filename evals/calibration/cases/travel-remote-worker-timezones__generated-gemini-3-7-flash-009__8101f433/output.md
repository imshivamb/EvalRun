### Time Zone & Work Schedule Matrix

* **Base Time Conversion**: Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30) to Korea Standard Time (KST, UTC+9:00) and Japan Standard Time (JST, UTC+9:00) is a difference of **+3 hours and 30 minutes**.
* **Weekday Availability Schedule (Monday – Friday)**:
  * **Free Sightseeing & Major Travel Window**: Wake-up until 4:00 PM KST/JST.
  * **Core Company Window**: 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM (Midnight) KST/JST (*12:30 PM – 8:30 PM IST*).
  * **Stationary Setup by**: **4:15 PM KST/JST** (*15 minutes prior to call*).
  * **Critical Meeting Block (Zero Transit / Stable Wi-Fi)**: **4:30 PM – 5:30 PM KST/JST** (*1:00 PM – 2:00 PM IST*).
  * **Flexible Work & Night Roaming Window**: 5:30 PM – 12:00 AM KST/JST (*Asynchronous work from cafés, vintage alleys, or hostels*).
* **Weekends (Saturday & Sunday)**: 100% free of work constraints for long day trips, early morning shoots, and regional transit.

## 28-Day Itinerary

### Week 1: South Korea (Seoul & Vicinity)
*Base: Hongdae / Yeonnam-dong (Hub for vintage shops, budget hostels, and late-night cafés).*

* **Day 1 (Mon) – Arrival in Seoul & Hongdae Orientation**
  * *Morning/Early Afternoon*: Arrive at Incheon (ICN), take AREX All-Stop train to Hongik University Station. Check in to hostel (e.g., Zzzip Guesthouse or Time Travelers Party Hostel).
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle in hostel co-working lounge.
  * *Evening*: Explore Hongdae street art alleys, street food along Eoulmadang-ro.
* **Day 2 (Tue) – Historic Seoul & Bukchon Photo Walk**
  * *08:00 – 15:30*: Gyeongbokgung Palace (arrive for guard change at 10:00 AM), wander Bukchon Hanok Village and Samcheong-dong art galleries.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Café Onion Anguk* or *Terarosa Coffee Gwanghwamun*.
  * *Evening*: Walk down Cheonggyecheon Stream; dinner at Gwangjang Market (Mungbean pancakes, Mayak gimbap).
* **Day 3 (Wed) – Seoul Modern Architecture & Vintage Thrifting**
  * *08:30 – 15:30*: Dongdaemun Design Plaza (Zaha Hadid architecture photography) and explore Dongmyo Flea Market (Seoul’s premier vintage clothing market).
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Return to Hongdae; settle at *Anthracite Coffee Roasters (Seogyo)*.
  * *Evening*: Vintage shopping along Yeonnam-dong’s Gyeongui Line Forest Park.
* **Day 4 (Thu) – Seongsu-dong ("Brooklyn of Seoul")**
  * *09:00 – 15:30*: Explore repurposed industrial warehouses, pop-up boutiques, and street art in Seongsu-dong.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Daelim Changgo Gallery Café* or *Center Coffee*.
  * *Evening*: Walk around Seoul Forest for sunset photography; async work at local café.
* **Day 5 (Fri) – Ihwa Mural Village & Naksan Park**
  * *08:30 – 15:30*: Hike the Seoul City Wall trail from Hyehwa-dong through Ihwa Mural Village to Naksan Park.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Hakrim Dabang* (historic café in Daehangno) or back at hostel.
  * *Evening*: Hongdae busking scene and late-night Korean BBQ.
* **Day 6 (Sat) – Weekend Free: Bukhansan National Park (No Work)**
  * *All Day*: Full-day hike up Bukhansan (Baegundae Peak, 836m) for panoramic mountain and autumn foliage views. Evening exploration of Myeongdong night market.
* **Day 7 (Sun) – Weekend Free: Incheon / Ferry or Flight to Japan (No Work)**
  * *Morning*: Chill morning in Mangwon Market and café hopping in Hapjeong.
  * *Afternoon/Evening*: Flight from Seoul (GMP/ICN) to Tokyo (HND/NRT). Check into Tokyo hostel (e.g., Nui. Hostel & Bar Lounge in Kuramae or CITAN in Nihonbashi).

  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Kayaba Coffee* (Yanaka) or *Nui. Hostel Lounge*.
  * *Evening*: Roam Akihabara backstreets; async work from an internet café or craft beer bar.
* **Day 9 (Tue) – Shimokitazawa (Tokyo's Vintage Capital)**
  * *09:00 – 15:30*: Explore Shimokitazawa’s maze of thrift shops (New York Joe Exchange, Chicago, Flamingo) and record stores.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Tag Café* or *Bookends Coffee Service* in Shimokitazawa.
  * *Evening*: Catch indie live music or dine at an alleyway izakaya.
* **Day 10 (Wed) – Koenji & Nakano Broadway**
  * *08:30 – 15:30*: Vintage military/Americana clothing stores in Koenji (Look Street), followed by subculture pop-culture hunting in Nakano Broadway.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *R-za Dokushokan* (quiet book café in Koenji) or *Muji Café Nakano*.

* **Day 11 (Thu) – Shibuya, Harajuku & Daikanyama**

* **Day 12 (Fri) – Ginza Architecture & Tsukiji Outer Market**
  * *08:00 – 15:30*: Fresh street food at Tsukiji Outer Market; photo walk admiring luxury modern architecture along Ginza and Marunouchi.
  * *16:15 – 17:30 [Meeting]*: Settle at *Doutor Coffee Ginza* or hostel workspace.
  * *Evening*: Night street photography at Yurakucho under-track dining alleys.
* **Day 13 (Sat) – Weekend Free: Kamakura & Enoshima Coastal Trip (No Work)**
  * *All Day*: Day trip on the Enoden line: Great Buddha of Kamakura, Hasedera Temple, and sunset over Mount Fuji from Enoshima Island.
* **Day 14 (Sun) – Weekend Free: Mount Takao Hike (No Work)**
  * *All Day*: Hike Trail 1/6 at Mount Takao (599m) for autumn foliage; evening onsen at Keio Takaosan Onsen Gokurakuyu.

### Practical Backpacker & Digital Nomad Guidelines

1. **Connectivity for Critical Video Calls**:
   * Rent an **unlimited 4G/5G Pocket Wi-Fi** or buy an **eSIM with unlimited high-speed data** (e.g., Ubigi, Airalo, or KT Roaming in Korea). Do not rely solely on public street Wi-Fi for your 4:30 PM meeting.
2. **Work Café Etiquette (Japan & Korea)**:
   * *Korea*: Cafés are extremely laptop-friendly; almost every seat has power outlets and high-speed Wi-Fi (often printed on the receipt).
   * *Japan*: Not all traditional kissaten allow laptops. Look for chains (*Starbucks, Tully's, Pronto*) or dedicated digital nomad spots (*FabCafe, Citan, Work-Cafés*) that explicitly offer power strips (`dengen`) and Wi-Fi.
3. **Budget Accommodations**:
   * Utilize reputable chain hostels with dedicated co-working areas (e.g., *Piece Hostels* in Kyoto, *K’s House* across Japan, *Zzzip/Time Travelers* in Seoul). Average dorm price: $20–$35 USD / night.
