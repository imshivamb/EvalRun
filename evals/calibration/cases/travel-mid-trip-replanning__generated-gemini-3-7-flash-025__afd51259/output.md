### **Cost-Reduction & Recovery Plan (Target: Save ~¥36,000 / ₹20,000)**

1. **Intercity Transit Savings (~¥9,000–¥11,000 saved):**
   - Instead of the standard Shinkansen from Kyoto to Tokyo (¥14,000+), take a daytime or overnight highway bus (e.g., *Willer Express* or *JR Bus*, typically ¥4,000–¥6,000 if booked a few days ahead).
2. **Airport Transit Savings (~¥2,000 saved):**
   - On Day 28, take the **Keisei Main Line Rapid** (approx. ¥1,050) from Nippori/Ueno to Narita Airport rather than the expensive Narita Express (¥3,070+) or Skyliner (¥2,570).
3. **Attraction & Viewpoint Swaps (~¥6,000 saved):**
   - Replace paid viewing decks (Shibuya Sky / Roppongi Hills) with the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building Observation Deck** in Shinjuku (100% free).
4. **Food Strategy (~¥15,000+ saved over 15 days):**
   - Take advantage of *Depachika* (department store basements) and supermarkets after 7:30 PM for 20–50% discounted bento boxes.
   - Utilize budget fast-casual chains (*Matsuya*, *Sukiya*, *Hanamaru Udon*) where hearty meals cost ¥450–¥700.

### **Day-by-Day Adapted Itinerary (Days 13–28)**

#### **Hiroshima & Transition to Kyoto**

* **Day 13: Hiroshima (Typhoon Indoor Day — No Miyajima)**
  * **Morning/Afternoon:** Stay sheltered from the storm. Explore the **Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum** (extended indoor visit; ¥200 entry). 
  * **Afternoon:** Head into the **Hondori Covered Arcade** (completely weatherproof) for budget vintage hunting (*2nd Street Hondori*, *Book-Off Super Bazaar*).
  * **Evening:** Dinner at **Okonomimura** (multi-floor indoor food hall) for an authentic, high-value Hiroshima-style Okonomiyaki (~¥900–¥1,200).
  * *Night:* Budget Hostel, Hiroshima.

* **Day 14: Hiroshima → Himeji → Kyoto (Check-in)**
  * **Morning:** *(Conditional: pending rail service clearance after the typhoon)* Take the JR train to Himeji. Store your backpack in the station coin lockers (approx. ¥500–¥700).
  * **Midday:** Walk up the tree-lined boulevard to photograph **Himeji Castle**. (To save budget, viewing and photographing the castle from the surrounding public park/courtyards is free; full interior entry is ¥1,000).
  * **Late Afternoon/Evening:** Continue via JR Special Rapid train to Kyoto. Check into your pre-paid, non-refundable hostel.

---

#### **Kyoto Base (Days 15–18 — Locked Accommodation)**

* **Day 15: Southern Kyoto & Covered Arcade Thrifting**
  * **Early Morning (06:30 AM):** Beat the crowds and mid-day heat at **Fushimi Inari Taisha** (Free entry; prime photography through the Senbon Torii).
  * **Afternoon:** Explore Kyoto’s vintage hubs around **Teramachi & Shin-Kyogoku Arcades** (*Chicago Vintage*, *Three Star Kyoto*). 
  * **Late Afternoon:** Grab an affordable drip coffee at a local *kissaten* (traditional Japanese café) like *Smart Coffee* or *Inoda Coffee*.

* **Day 16: Arashiyama & Western Kyoto (Low-Cost Photography)**
  * **Morning:** Early morning walk through the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (Free) and photograph the **Togetsukyo Bridge**.
  * **Afternoon:** Walk along the scenic, residential Sagano rural path toward the preserved streets of **Saga-Toriimoto** (Free street photography).
  * **Evening:** Relax by the Kamo River banks with a convenience store snack and watch the evening city lights.

* **Day 17: Northern Higashiyama Cultural Walk**
  * **Morning:** Walk the **Philosopher's Path** (*Tetsugaku-no-Michi*) and visit the brick aqueduct at **Nanzen-ji Temple** (grounds are free to photograph).
  * **Evening:** Sunset street photography through the historic preservation lanes of **Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka** and **Gion** (exterior photography only; observe photography restriction signs in private alleys).

* **Day 18: Kyoto Thrift Crawl & Pack Up**
  * **Day:** Visit suburban thrift shops (*2nd Street Kyoto Oki*, *Hard-Off*) for lower prices than central tourist shops.
  * **Evening:** Pick up discounted supermarket bento boxes for dinner; rest up for the long transit day to Tokyo.

---

#### **Tokyo (Days 19–28)**

* **Day 19: Kyoto to Tokyo (Budget Highway Transit)**
  * **Daytime:** Depart Kyoto on a budget highway bus *(Willer Express / JR Bus)* to Tokyo (approx. 7–8 hours; saves substantial money compared to the Shinkansen).
  * **Evening:** Check into Tokyo hostel. Take a free evening photography walk around the neon-lit alleyways of **Omoide Yokocho** and **Kabukicho** in Shinjuku.

* **Day 20: Tokyo Thrifting — Shimokitazawa**
  * **Day:** Full day exploring **Shimokitazawa**. Hunt through curated vintage shops (*Flamingo*, *New York Joe Exchange*, *Stick Out* — where select racks are ¥700–¥800).
  * **Cafés:** Grab a budget pour-over at an indie roastery like *Bear Pond Espresso* or *Frankie Tokyo*.

* **Day 21: Tokyo Thrifting & Subculture — Koenji & Nakano**
  * **Morning/Afternoon:** Thrift shopping in **Koenji** (known for authentic streetwear, punk, and vintage Americana at *Sokkyou*, *Whistler*, and *2nd Street Koenji*).
  * **Late Afternoon:** Walk to **Nakano Broadway** for retro camera shops, vintage watches, and inexpensive basement food stalls.

* **Day 22: High-Altitude Photography & Harajuku (Replacement for Closed teamLab)**
  * **Morning:** Visit the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** in Shinjuku for free panoramic views across Tokyo (and Mt. Fuji on clear days).
  * **Afternoon:** Walk through **Meiji Jingu Shrine** (Free) into **Harajuku & Cat Street** for window shopping, thrifting (*Kinji Used Clothing*), and street fashion photography.

* **Day 23: Old Tokyo "Shitamachi" Photography**
  * **Morning:** Explore **Yanaka Ginza**, one of Tokyo’s last remaining historic merchant neighborhoods. Photograph the retro storefronts and try local street food (croquettes for ~¥150).
  * **Afternoon:** Stroll through **Ueno Park** and the budget-friendly **Ameyoko Open-Air Market**.

* **Day 24: Rescheduled teamLab Planets & Odaiba Waterfront**
  * *(Conditional: Contact teamLab customer support / visit the ticketing portal to reschedule your original Day 22 ticket to Day 24, as emergency maintenance ends after Day 23).*
  * **Afternoon:** Visit **teamLab Planets** in Toyosu.
  * **Evening:** Walk to the **Odaiba Seaside Park** to photograph the Rainbow Bridge, the miniature Statue of Liberty, and the Tokyo skyline at night.

* **Day 25: Akihabara & Asakusa Night Photography**
  * **Afternoon:** Explore the electronics lanes and retro gaming alleys of **Akihabara**.
  * **Evening:** Head to **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa after dark (the crowds disperse, the temple is illuminated, and the grounds are free to wander and photograph).

* **Day 26: Shibuya & Meguro River Walk**
  * **Morning/Afternoon:** Walk through **Daikanyama** and along the **Meguro River** (café culture and independent boutique photography).
  * **Evening:** Photograph the famous **Shibuya Scramble Crossing** from the 2nd-floor crossing corridors of Shibuya Station (Free view).

* **Day 27: Final Thrifting & Tokyo Souvenirs**
  * **Day:** Revisit your favorite thrifting spots for final purchases or browse **Mega Don Quijote Shibuya** for low-cost souvenir snacks (Kit-Kats, matcha).
  * **Evening:** Final night photo session around **Shinjuku Golden Gai** (exterior streetscapes).

* **Day 28: Tokyo Departure (Narita Airport - NRT)**
  * **Morning:** Check out of your hostel.
  * **Transit:** Take the Yamanote Line to Nippori Station, then transfer to the **Keisei Main Line Limited Express** directly to Narita Airport (approx. 75 min, ¥1,050).
  * **Afternoon:** Arrive at NRT at least 3 hours prior to your non-changeable flight home.
