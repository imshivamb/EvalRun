### **Revised Itinerary: Day 13 to Day 28**

**Day 13: Hiroshima (Typhoon Reroute)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** With ferries canceled and a typhoon warning in effect, stay indoors and safe. Head to the **Hondori Covered Shopping Arcade**. It is completely roofed and safe from heavy weather.
*   **Activity:** Hit up the thrift stores located in and around Hondori (e.g., *Kinji Used Clothing*, *2nd Street*, and *Spinns*). Enjoy long, slow coffee breaks at local cafés like *Obscura Coffee Roasters* to wait out the rain.
*   **Evening:** Eat at **Okonomimura**, a multi-story indoor food park serving cheap and filling Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki.

**Day 14: Hiroshima → Himeji → Kyoto (Budget Transit Day)**
*   **Morning:** Check out and take the JR Sanyo Main Line (local/rapid trains) from Hiroshima to Himeji. This takes about 4.5 hours but costs a fraction of the Shinkansen. 
*   **Afternoon:** Stash your backpack in a coin locker at Himeji Station. Walk down Otemae Street to **Himeji Castle**. *Budget Tip:* Do not pay the ¥1,000 entry fee. The absolute best photography spots are from the free outer grounds and the adjacent Kokoen Garden perimeter.
*   **Evening:** Take the JR Special Rapid train (approx. 90 mins) from Himeji to Kyoto. Check into your fully pre-paid, non-refundable budget guesthouse.

**Days 15–18: Kyoto (Locked Dates - Free Exploration)**
*   **Day 15:** Early morning photography at **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (free entry). Spend the afternoon exploring cafés in the Nakagyo Ward.
*   **Day 16:** **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** at dawn (free). Walk around the Togetsukyo Bridge area and grab cheap convenience store matcha treats instead of expensive tourist snacks.
*   **Day 17:** Thrift shopping day in Kyoto. Walk around the Teramachi Street covered arcade; check out *Chicago* (famous vintage store) and *Three Star Kyoto*. 
*   **Day 18:** Photograph the historic Higashiyama district (Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka) in the morning light. Enjoy the architecture and window shopping (all free).

**Day 19: Kyoto → Tokyo (Budget Transit)**
*   **Transit Shift:** Cancel/skip the Shinkansen. Take a Willer Express or JR Highway Bus to Tokyo. A daytime bus takes about 8 hours, acts as a sightseeing tour from your window, and saves you roughly ¥9,000 (₹5,000).

**Day 20: Tokyo (Harajuku & Shibuya)**
