### **Strategic Budget Mitigation Plan (~¥35,000 / ₹20,000 Saved)**
To offset the unexpected ₹20,000 expense without sacrificing the quality of your experience, we have implemented the following cost-saving adjustments:
1. **Transit Swap (Save ~¥9,000 / ₹5,200):** Replaced the Kyoto-to-Tokyo Shinkansen bullet train (¥14,000) with a daytime highway bus (e.g., Willer Express, ~¥5,000). 
2. **Airport Express Swap (Save ~¥1,700 / ₹1,000):** On Day 28, utilize the Keisei Access Express (¥1,380) instead of the Skyliner or Narita Express (¥2,600+).
3. **Observatory Swap (Save ~¥2,200 / ₹1,300):** Substituted paid Tokyo observation decks (like Shibuya Sky) with the free observation deck at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building.
4. **Food & Drink Strategy (Save ~¥22,500 / ₹13,000 over 15 days):** Allocate ¥1,500 less per day by eating breakfast/lunch at highly rated convenience stores (7-Eleven/Lawson) or budget-friendly Japanese fast-food chains (Matsuya, Yoshinoya, Sukiya) and limiting café visits to one cheap, curated local specialty coffee per day.

### **Day-by-Day Revised Itinerary (Days 13–28)**

#### **Day 13: Typhoon Safety Day (Hiroshima)**
*Due to the typhoon warning and Miyajima ferry cancellations, transit should be minimized. This day focuses on indoor, walking-distance shelter.*
* **Morning**: Sleep in and monitor local weather alerts. Once safe, head to the **Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum** (Entry: ¥200—extremely budget-friendly and fully indoors).
* **Afternoon**: Explore the **Hondori Shaded Arcade**, a fully covered shopping street safe from wind and rain. Explore local thrift stores like *2nd Street Hondori* for budget vintage finds.
* **Dinner**: Head to **Okonomimura** (Okonomiyaki Village) nearby. It’s an indoor building with over 20 stalls serving Hiroshima-style Okonomiyaki (filling, delicious, and budget-friendly at ~¥1,000–¥1,500 per meal).
* *Overnight*: Hiroshima budget hostel (Pre-booked).

#### **Day 14: Hiroshima to Kyoto via Himeji**
* **Morning**: Take the morning train to **Himeji** (Ensure train lines have resumed normal operations post-typhoon). 
* **Afternoon**: Walk to **Himeji Castle**. To save the ¥1,000 entry fee, take stunning, professional-grade photos of the castle exterior for free from the surrounding **Sannomaru Square** and castle park grounds. 
* **Evening**: Continue to Kyoto. Check into your pre-paid, non-refundable Kyoto guesthouse. 
* *Overnight*: Kyoto Guesthouse (Pre-paid).

#### **Day 15: Kyoto (Fushimi Inari & Shinkyogoku Thrifting)**
* **Morning**: Wake up early (6:30 AM) to catch the sunrise at **Fushimi Inari Shrine**. Walking up the mountain through the thousands of Torii gates is completely **free** and highly photogenic.
* **Afternoon**: Spend the afternoon exploring the **Teramachi and Shinkyogoku Covered Arcades**. These arcades host incredible vintage clothing stores like *Three Star Kyoto* and *2nd Street*, offering affordable Japanese vintage garments.
* **Cafe Stop**: Grab a budget-friendly matcha latte at a local, minimalist cafe like *Weekenders Coffee* (tucked away in a parking lot, great photo spot).
* *Overnight*: Kyoto Guesthouse (Pre-paid).

#### **Day 16: Kyoto (Arashiyama & Local Cafés)**
* **Morning**: Visit the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** (Free) early to beat the crowds. Walk across the **Togetsukyo Bridge** (Free) for sweeping river photography.
* **Afternoon**: Visit the nearby **Kimono Forest** (Free) at Arashiyama Station, which features colorful acrylic pillars displaying traditional kimono patterns. 
* **Evening**: Head back to central Kyoto and stroll through the narrow, historic streets of **Pontocho Alley** at dusk (Free to walk, incredible for low-light photography).
* *Overnight*: Kyoto Guesthouse (Pre-paid).

#### **Day 17: Kyoto (Historic Higashiyama Walk)**
* **Morning**: Take a self-guided walking tour through **Higashiyama District** (Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka streets). The historic wooden buildings are free to admire.
* **Afternoon**: Visit **Yasaka Shrine** (Free) and walk through the historic **Gion District**, keeping an eye out for Geishas (remember to respect local photography restrictions here).
* **Budget Dinner**: Grab dinner at *Chao Chao Gyoza* or a local udon shop (approx. ¥800–¥1,200).
* *Overnight*: Kyoto Guesthouse (Pre-paid).

#### **Day 18: Kyoto (Nishiki Market & Vintage Hunting)**
* **Morning**: Browse **Nishiki Market** (Free to enter). Walk through to enjoy the sights, smells, and sample inexpensive skewers (¥200-¥400).
* **Afternoon**: Dedicate the afternoon to any remaining thrift shops in the Sanjo area. Alternatively, sit with a book or camera along the scenic banks of the **Kamogawa River** (a local favorite free activity).
* *Overnight*: Kyoto Guesthouse (Pre-paid).

#### **Day 19: Kyoto to Tokyo (The Major Budget Save)**
* **Morning/Afternoon**: Check out of Kyoto. Board a daytime **Highway Bus** (such as Willer Express, departing Kyoto Station around 8:00 AM or 9:00 AM, arriving in Tokyo in the late afternoon). 
  * *Why*: While it takes ~7.5 hours, it features comfortable reclining seats, free Wi-Fi, and power outlets. More importantly, it costs only **¥4,500–¥6,000**, saving you nearly **¥8,000–¥9,000 (approx. ₹5,000)** over the Shinkansen.
* **Evening**: Arrive in Tokyo. Check into your Tokyo hostel.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 20: Tokyo (Classic Shibuya & Free Skyline)**
* **Morning**: Explore **Harajuku’s Takeshita Street** and walk down **Omotesando** for modern architecture photography.
* **Afternoon**: Head to **Shibuya Crossing**. Walk across and capture photos from the second-floor windows of the Shibuya Station corridor (Free).
* **Evening**: Take the subway to Shinjuku. Visit the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building Observatory** in Nishi-Shinjuku. The 45th-floor observation deck offers spectacular, panoramic views of the Tokyo skyline entirely for **free** (saving you the ¥2,200 Shibuya Sky entry fee).
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 21: Tokyo (Vintage Shopping in Shimokitazawa)**
* **Morning/Afternoon**: Spend the day in **Shimokitazawa**, the capital of vintage shopping in Tokyo. 
  * *Budget Thrifting spots*: Visit *Toyo Department Store* (a garage-like space filled with small thrift stalls), *New York Joe Exchange*, and *Kinji*. You can find high-quality vintage tees and jackets for as low as ¥1,000–¥2,000.
* **Cafe Stop**: Enjoy a budget-friendly drip coffee at one of Shimokitazawa’s indie espresso stands.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 22: Tokyo (Shinjuku Street Photography - *teamLab Swap*)**
* *Note: teamLab Planets is closed today for emergency maintenance.*
* **Morning**: Visit **Meiji Jingu Shrine** (Free) located inside a quiet, massive forest in the middle of Tokyo.
* **Afternoon**: Walk through the nearby **Yoyogi Park** (Free)—great for people-watching and street photography.
* **Night**: Head to **Shinjuku**. Photograph the neon lights of **Kabukicho** and the narrow, atmospheric alleys of **Omoide Yokocho** (Memory Lane). 
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 23: Tokyo (Vintage Shopping in Koenji)**
* **Morning/Afternoon**: Head to **Koenji**, Tokyo’s gritty, subcultural thrift haven. Koenji is generally cheaper and less touristy than Shimokitazawa.
  * *Spots to visit*: Look for bargains along the *Pal Shopping Arcade* and explore *Slut* or *Sokkyou* for vintage Americana and retro fashion.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 24: Tokyo (Rescheduled teamLab Planets)**
* **Morning/Afternoon**: **teamLab Planets (Toyosu)**. (Use your pre-purchased ticket, which has been successfully rescheduled for today now that the facility has reopened). Enjoy the immersive digital art projection.
* **Late Afternoon**: Walk around the waterfront of **Odaiba** to see the Giant Unicorn Gundam Statue and get a sunset photo of the Rainbow Bridge.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 25: Tokyo (Traditional Asakusa & Akihabara)**
* **Morning**: Visit **Senso-ji Temple** in Asakusa (Free). Explore the Nakamise shopping street.
* **Afternoon**: Walk across the Sumida River to check out the exterior architecture of the Asahi Beer Headquarters.
* **Evening**: Head to **Akihabara** to photograph the bright anime billboards and neon-drenched streets at dusk.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 26: Tokyo (Quiet Nakameguro & Daikanyama)**
* **Morning**: Stroll along the **Meguro River Canal** in Nakameguro. 
* **Afternoon**: Window shop in **Daikanyama**, known as the "Brooklyn of Tokyo". Visit *T-Site Daikanyama*, one of the most beautiful bookstores in the world, perfect for photography and relaxed reading.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 27: Tokyo (Souvenir Hunting & Last Night)**
* **Morning/Afternoon**: Spend the day gathering budget-friendly souvenirs at **Ameyoko Market** in Ueno or a multi-story **Don Quijote** store (where you can find cheap Japanese snacks/KitKats to take home).
* **Evening**: Pack your bags and enjoy a final budget dinner of ramen or sushi conveyor belt plates.
* *Overnight*: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 28: Tokyo (Departure via Narita Airport)**
* **Morning**: Check out of your hostel. 
* **Transit**: Walk to Ueno Station or Nippori Station. Board the **Keisei Access Express** directly to Narita Airport (NRT). 
  * *Cost*: ¥1,380 (Takes about 60 minutes from Nippori—saving you nearly 50% compared to the Skyliner or Narita Express).
* **Afternoon**: Check in at NRT for your locked, non-changeable flight home. Safe travels!
