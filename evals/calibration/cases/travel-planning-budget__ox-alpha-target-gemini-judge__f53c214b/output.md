## 3. Before You Book

- **Visas (conditional — verify current rules):** Japan e-visa for Indians goes through designated authorized agents; single-entry fees have been temporarily waived. Korea: Indians are covered by a temporary K-ETA exemption (recently extended), but confirm on the **official K-ETA site only** (clone sites overcharge).
- **Ferry Busan→Fukuoka:** Book 1–2 weeks ahead on official Camellia Line / JR Beetle sites (passport required). Sailings cancel in rough seas — keep a fallback Peach/LCC flight Busan→Fukuoka/Kansai (~₹4–7k) in mind. *(Schedule/price: verify.)*
- **Apps/cards:** Install **Suica on iPhone Wallet** (works globally; Android needs a Japanese phone), **T-money/WOWpass** in Korea, **Visit Japan Web** (immigration QR), Organic Maps offline, Google Translate camera mode.
- **Cash:** Cards work almost everywhere in cities; withdraw yen at 7-Eleven ATMs (foreign-card friendly) via a zero-markup card (Wise/Revolut) — regular Indian cards bleed on forex.

## 4. PART ONE — SOUTH KOREA (Days 1–8)

Base 1: guesthouse in **Hongdae/Sinchon** (5N) → Base 2: **Haeundae or Seomyeon**, Busan (2N).

**Day 1 — Arrive Seoul**
- AREX from Incheon to Hongik Univ (all-stop ~₩5,000 / express ~₩11,000). Check in, recover.
- Evening: **Gyeongui Line Forest Park** at golden hour (elevated linear park, locals picnicking), then Hongdae street performances.
- Eat: gimbap + tteokbokki stall, ₩5–8k. Photo pick: park benches against sunset towers.

**Day 2 — Palaces & hanok core**
- Rent a hanbok (~₩15–25k/2hrs) → **Gyeongbokgung entry becomes free** + your best portrait shots; catch the guard-changing ceremony (10:00).
- Walk **Bukchon Hanok Village** early (before tour groups), then **Samcheong-dong** café row and **Onion Anguk** (café in a restored hanok).
- Afternoon: **Ikseon-dong** — hanok alleys, vintage shops, dessert cafés (Cheongsudang). Evening: **Cheonggyecheon stream** walk + dinner at **Gwangjang Market** (bindaetteok, mayak gimbap, ~₩10k).

**Day 3 — Seongsu-dong ("Seoul's Brooklyn")**
- Full day in **Seongsu**: repurposed factory cafés (**Onion Seongsu**), vintage stores (Sunday Market and neighbors), pop-up streets.
- Sunset: **Ttukseom Hangang Park** → Han River picnic (CU tents, ramen machines — peak local-culture moment, ₩5–10k). Night: bridge reflections on the Han.
- *This is a low-spend, high-reward day.*

**Day 4 — Mangwon & the northwest**
- Morning: **Mangwon Market** (cheap eats, ₩5k breakfast crawl) + Mangridan-gil café alleys.
- Afternoon: **Haneul Park (World Cup Park)** — climb for the definitive Seoul sunset panorama.
- Evening: **Yeonnam-dong** backstreets behind the Gyeongui park.

**Day 5 — Thrift day + old walls**
- **Dongmyo Flea Market** — Seoul's best second-hand sprawl (best Sat–Sun; if your date is a weekday, substitute indoor **Eulji Myeongpum** vintage department in Euljiro). *(Hours: verify for your weekday.)*
- Walk **Naksan Fortress Wall → Ihwa Mural Village** at sunset. Evening: **Euljiro "Hipjiro"** — print-shop alleys turned bars, nogari (dried pollock) + beer.
- If Saturday: Hongdae **Free Market** (Apr–Nov) for handmade goods.

**Day 6 — Seoul → Busan**
- KTX ~2h40m (₩59,800; book on Korail app). Stay **Haeundae**.
- Evening: **Gwangalli Beach** — Gwangan Bridge illumination, drone shows some nights *(verify)*.

**Day 7 — Busan greatest hits**
- Sunrise/morning: **Gamcheon Culture Village** in soft light (avoid midday crowds).
- **Jagalchi Fish Market** lunch (raw fish upstairs, grilled downstairs), **BIFF Square**, **Yongdusan Park**.
- Late afternoon: **Huinnyeoul Culture Village** — whitewashed cliff village above the sea; café with a wave-view window.
- Night: **Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market** or Seomyeon food alleys.

**Day 8 — Ferry to Japan**
- Morning: last Busan wander + luggage storage at station/port.
- **Camellia Line ferry Busan ~12:00 → Hakata ~16:30** (same timezone). Shared berth keeps cost down (~¥9–16k depending on vessel/class — verify current fares).
- Evening: **Nakasu yatai riverside food stalls** — Fukuoka's signature experience; queue at a stall with locals (ramen ¥1–1.5k).

## 5. PART TWO — JAPAN (Days 8–29)

**Day 9 — Fukuoka / Dazaifu**
- Nishitetsu line to **Dazaifu** (¥420 each way): Tenmangu shrine approach lined with third-wave roasters and mochi grills (umegae mochi ~¥150); **Ichiran's original ramen shop** is here.
- Afternoon back in town: **Ohori Park** loop, **Yanagibashi Union Market** (local kitchen-market vibe).
- Evening: Tenjin arcades + **Daimyo** backstreets (vintage shops, bars).

**Day 10 — To Hiroshima**
- Shinkansen Nozomi ~1h10m (~¥9,500). Stay near Hiroshima Station.
- Afternoon: **Peace Memorial Park + Museum** (¥200; allow 2–3 hrs — emotionally essential). Dusk: **Atomic Bomb Dome** across the Motoyasu River (blue-hour reflection shots).

**Day 11 — Miyajima**
- JR ferry (~¥180–220 with IC card). **Itsukushima floating torii** at high tide *(check tide table)*, then skip the crowds up to **Daisho-in** — the island's most atmospheric temple complex.
- Hike or ropeway toward **Mt. Misen** (hike up ~90 min, free). Grilled oysters + momiji manju along the lanes.
- Return: dinner at **Okonomimura** — 4 floors of Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki counters (~¥1,200–1,800).

**Day 12 — To Kyoto (base: Demachiyanagi/Nishijin or near Nishiki)**
- Shinkansen to Kyoto ~1h40m (~¥12,000). Check in (5 nights — unpack properly).
- Golden hour: **Kamo River delta** at Demachiyanagi; dusk: **Pontochō** lantern lanes.

**Day 13 — Eastern Kyoto at speed**
- **Fushimi Inari at 6:30–7:00** — empty vermilion tunnels (your portfolio shot). Free entry.
- Chain: Tofukuji → Sanjusangendo (1,001 statues, ¥600) → **Kiyomizu-dera** (¥400) via Sannenzaka before 9am → Kodai-ji → Yasaka Shrine → **Gion/Shirakawa canal** at dusk.
- Coffee: **% Arabica Higashiyama** on the pagoda lane.

**Day 14 — Arashiyama + northwest**
- Bamboo Grove before 8am, then pay for **Ōkōchi Sansō villa garden** (¥1,300, matcha included — the quiet anti-crowd gem). Saga-Toriimoto preserved street lunch (yudofu/soba).
- Afternoon: bus to **Kinkakuji** (golden pavilion, ¥500) or rest; evening riverside drink on the Kamo banks.

**Day 15 — Kyoto craft & café day (work-friendly)**
- **Nishiki Market** breakfast crawl → **Teramachi/Shinmonzen** antique + vintage streets (**Chicago Kyoto** for second-hand) → **Demachi Masugata** shotengai.
- Café circuit: **Weekenders Coffee** (hidden stand-up bar), **Smart Coffee** (1948 kissaten hotcakes), **Kurasu**.
- Evening free — good night to absorb meeting overflow (see work playbook).

**Day 16 — Nara day trip**
- Kintetsu from Kyoto (~¥620–760 each way, 35–45 min). **Todai-ji Great Buddha** (¥600), bowing deer, **Kasuga Taisha** lantern paths, **Naramachi** machiya quarter.
- Optional: Wakakusayama hill viewpoint. Back in Kyoto by evening.

**Day 17 — Kyoto → Osaka (base: Nakazakichō or Namba)**
- JR special rapid ¥580, 30 min. Check in (3 nights).
- Afternoon: **Nakazakichō** — surviving Taishō-era houses turned into cafés and vintage shops (your aesthetic, exactly).
- Night: **Dotombori neon** (Glico-sign shot from Ebisubashi) + **Ura-Namba** izakaya alleys.

**Day 18 — Osaka retro circuit**
- **Tenjinbashisuji Arcade** — Japan's longest covered shopping street (2.6 km): retro kissaten, resale shops, ¥500 lunches.
- Afternoon: **Amerikamura** streetwear/vintage (**Big Step**) + **Orange Street** boutiques.
- Sunset: **Umeda Sky Building** (¥1,500). Night: **Shinsekai** — Tsutenkaku tower, kushikatsu, Janjan Yokocho's fading-showa glow.

**Day 19 — Flexible day → overnight bus**
- Option A (default): slow morning, **Sumiyoshi Taisha** (unique straight-roof shrine, few tourists), pack, work block.
- Option B swap: half-day **Himeji Castle** (rapid train ~1h each way, ¥1,560; entry ~¥1,100) — Japan's finest castle.
- **23:00 overnight bus to Tokyo** (¥8–13k; book via Willer/Bus-Channel — saves a hotel night + a daylight hour). Comfort upgrade alternative: morning Nozomi ¥14,400 if you need real sleep before a work week.

**Day 20 — Tokyo arrival (base: Kuramae/Asakusa, 9 nights)**
- Arrive ~07:00; bag-drop at hostel, shower, coffee. Slow day: **Sumida riverside walk**, **Kappabashi** kitchenware street.
- Early night — you slept on a bus.

**Day 21 — Old Tokyo**
- **Sensō-ji at 5:30–6:00** — incense smoke in empty lantern light. Side streets: Demboin-dori.
- Walk to **Yanaka Ginza** shotengai + cemetery lanes + **Kayaba Coffee** (century-old kissa). **Nezu Shrine** torii tunnel.
- Evening: **Ameyoko** market chaos, Ueno.

**Day 22 — Shimokitazawa thrift marathon**
- Full day: **New York Joe Exchange, Chicago, Flamingo, Haight & Ashbury** + independent racks. Coffee: **Bear Pond Espresso** (cash only, cult status — verify hours).
- Evening: **Shibuya Scramble** from the free Magnet rooftop, or paid **Shibuya Sky** deck (book ahead, ~¥2,200–2,700).

**Day 23 — Koenji + Nakano (underground Tokyo)**
- **Koenji**: dense cluster of vintage dens around the north exit; look, don't rush.
- **Nakano Broadway**: Mandarake collector floors (even if you buy nothing, it's a museum of subculture).
- Night: **Golden Gai** — one drink in a six-seat bar (~¥1,500) or **Omoide Yokocho** yakitori smoke.

**Day 24 — Kamakura + Enoshima day trip**
- JR Yokosuka line ~1h (~¥950 each way). **Kotoku-in Great Buddha** (¥300), Komachi-dōri cafés, **Hase-dera** terrace view (¥400).
- Ride the **Enoden** coastal tram (day pass ¥800) to **Enoshima** — sunset over the water; Fuji silhouette if skies cooperate.

**Day 25 — Harajuku → Omotesando → Yoyogi**
- **Meiji Shrine** forest at opening (free). People-watch **Takeshita-dori**, then **Cat Street** vintage (RagTag, Kinji) into **Omotesando** architecture walk (Ando/Kuma facades).
- Sunset: **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** free observatory. Evening: **Nakameguro canal** stroll + **Onibus Coffee**.

**Day 26 — Built-in work/buffer day**
- Morning calls from a private booth; afternoon local loop: **Kuramae** stationery/leather craft (Kakimori ink bar), **Blue Bottle Kiyosumi** warehouse roastery, **Kiyosumi Gardens** (¥150).
- Laundry reset. This day absorbs weather delays or fatigue debt.

**Day 27 — Nikko or Mt. Takao (choose by forecast)**
- Clear day: **Mt. Takao** hike (~¥400 train each way) — early-autumn color up top, Fuji views. *(Foliage timing: verify for late Oct.)*
- Cultural day: **Nikko** via Tobu round-trip deal from Asakusa (~¥2,000–2,700) — Toshogu (¥1,300) + **Kanmangafuchi Abyss** row of stone Jizō (quietly spectacular, free). *(Autumn peak mid–late Oct at elevation — verify.)*

**Day 28 — Market morning + books + farewell views**
- **Tsukiji Outer Market** breakfast (many stalls close Wednesdays/Sundays — anchor to your date). *(Verify.)*
- **Ginza** architecture → **Jimbocho** secondhand book quarter (Isseido, Kitazawa — a whole district of used books) → Tokyo Station Marunouchi facade.
- Final night: **Zojōji temple with Tokyo Tower** behind it — the closing frame.

**Day 29 — Depart**
- Monorail from Hamamatsucho (if Haneda) or Skyliner (if Narita). Last konbini run; fly home.

## 6. Remote-Work Playbook

- **Time zones:** Japan/Korea are IST +3:30. Take calls **early IST morning (= 9:30–11:30 am local)**; protect evenings for exploring.
- **Best infrastructure:** Korea wins — 24-hour **study cafés (스터디카페)** in Hongdae/Sinchon offer private booths (~₩1,500/hr) made for calls. Japan: chain cafés (Starbucks/Tully's/Doutor) tolerate laptops; many indie cafés ban them (watch for signs). Co-working day passes run ¥1,000–2,000.
- **Choose hostels explicitly for lounge + desk + fast upload speeds** (read recent reviews mentioning video calls). Your Kyoto and Tokyo stays are long enough to negotiate weekly rates — ask directly; it routinely saves 10–20%.
- **Kit:** universal adapter (Type A/C), 65W GaN charger, short extension board, phone gimbal/travel tripod, and a 20,000mAh battery.

## 7. Levers If You Overspend

1. Swap the Osaka→Tokyo bus decision both ways (already built in).
2. Cook 2–3 hostel-kitchen dinners/week (−₹6–8k).
3. Skip Shibuya Sky and teamLab; use free decks (TMG, Magnet) (−₹4k).
4. Extend Tokyo weekly-rate negotiation to Osaka (−₹2–4k).
5. Downgrade one shinkansen leg to a daytime highway bus (−₹4k, +half a day).

## 8. October Notes & Contingencies

- **Weather:** Seoul/Tokyo/Kyoto 14–23°C, mostly dry, cool evenings. Pack layers + a light shell. Typhoon risk is tapering but nonzero — keep Days 26–28 swappable.
- **Date-anchored events (verify once you fix dates):** Kyoto **Jidai Matsuri + Kurama Fire Festival, Oct 22** — if your Kyoto block can cover Oct 22, do it; the Kurama fire festival is a photographer's dream (expect packed Eizan line trains). **Shibuya Halloween (Oct 31)** — legendary crowd chaos, alcohol bans enforced; fun to witness, plan exit routes.
- **Weekly closures to respect:** many Japanese museums close Mondays (e.g., Tokyo National Museum); Tsukiji Outer Market thins Wed/Sun; Dongmyo flea market peaks Sat/Sun. Once your dates are fixed, anchor Days 5, 21, 22, 28 to the right weekdays.
- **Safety net:** keep ~₹5k buffer untouched; both countries are extremely safe for solo travel (emergency: 112 Korea / 119 Japan).
