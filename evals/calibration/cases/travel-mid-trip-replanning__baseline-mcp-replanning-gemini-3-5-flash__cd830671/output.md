### 🗺️ Revised Day-by-Day Itinerary (Days 13–28)

#### **Day 13: Hiroshima (Typhoon Safety Day)**
*   **Activity**: Stay safe and dry indoors. Avoid all coastal areas. Explore the **Hondori Shopping Arcade**, a massive, fully-covered pedestrian street lined with budget-friendly local cafes, thrift stores, and arcade centers.
*   **Dining**: Try Hiroshima-style Okonomiyaki at Okonomimura (a multi-story building of cheap, filling Okonomiyaki stalls). It’s highly budget-friendly (~¥1,000–¥1,500) and completely indoors.
*   **Logistics**: Keep walking to a minimum and monitor local transport announcements.
*   **Accommodation**: Budget Hostel, Hiroshima (Same as Day 12).

#### **Day 14: Hiroshima ➔ Himeji Castle ➔ Kyoto**
*   **Morning**: Take the JR train to Himeji. *(Conditional: If Shinkansen services are delayed/suspended in the morning due to leftover typhoon effects, delay departure to afternoon).*
*   **Afternoon**: Visit **Himeji Castle**. To save money, you can skip the inner castle tower ticket (¥1,000) and photograph the stunning exterior from the surrounding Sannomaru Square gardens for free. 
*   **Evening**: Take the JR Special Rapid Service (cheaper than Shinkansen, ¥2,310) from Himeji directly to Kyoto. 
*   **Accommodation**: Pre-paid Kyoto Guesthouse (Checked-in).

#### **Days 15–18: Kyoto (Locked-in pre-paid dates)**
*   **Day 15 (East Kyoto & Shrines)**: Walk the traditional streets of **Higashiyama** and visit **Fushimi Inari Shrine** (Free, open 24/7). Go late in the afternoon or at night for dramatic, crowd-free photography.
*   **Day 16 (Arashiyama & Thrifting)**: Visit the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove** and **Kimono Forest** (both Free) in the morning. In the afternoon, head to the Teramachi and Shinkyogoku covered arcades for budget vintage shopping at stores like *Three Star Kyoto* and *2nd Street*.
*   **Day 17 (Cafes & Kamo River)**: Cafe hopping day. Visit retro Japanese *kissaten* (traditional coffee shops) like *Smart Coffee* or *Cafe Independent* for cheap lunch sets. Spend the afternoon walking and taking photos along the **Kamo River** (Free).
*   **Day 18 (North Kyoto & Prep)**: Visit **Yasaka Shrine** and the historic Gion district (Free to walk around). Buy snacks and drinks at a local supermarket (cheaper than convenience stores) to prep for your bus journey tomorrow.
*   **Accommodation**: Pre-paid Kyoto Guesthouse.

#### **Day 19: Kyoto ➔ Tokyo (Budget Transit Day)**
*   **Morning/Afternoon**: Check out of Kyoto. Board a budget **daytime highway bus** (e.g., Willer Express, departing Kyoto Station around 8:00 AM, arriving in Tokyo around 4:30 PM). This long drive is scenic and saves you massive transit costs.
*   **Evening**: Check into your Tokyo hostel. Take a free night-photography walk around the neon-lit streets of **Shinjuku** (Omoide Yokocho and Kabukicho).
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 20: Tokyo (Vintage Shopping in Shimokitazawa)**
*   **Activity**: Spend the day in **Shimokitazawa**, Tokyo's vintage capital. It's incredibly atmospheric and free to window-shop. Check out budget-friendly thrift chains like *Toyo Department Store* and *Chicago*.
*   **Logistics**: Activate your first **24-Hour Tokyo Subway Ticket** (¥800) to cover all subway travel.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 21: Tokyo (Subculture & Retro Gaming in Koenji & Nakano)**
*   **Activity**: Explore **Koenji**, a neighborhood famous for cheap punk-vintage clothing, thrift stores, and vinyl records. In the afternoon, walk to **Nakano Broadway** to view retro toys, manga, and vintage video games.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 22: Tokyo (Shinjuku, Harajuku & Free Skyline Views)**
*   **Activity**: Walk through the serene **Meiji Shrine** (Free), then dive into the colorful chaos of **Harajuku (Takeshita Street)**. In the late afternoon, head to the **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building** in Shinjuku for stunning, free panoramic views of the city skyline at sunset.
*   **Note**: This was your original teamLab day; it has been swapped to keep you away from Toyosu during the museum's emergency maintenance.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 23: Tokyo (Asakusa & Ueno)**
*   **Activity**: Visit Tokyo’s oldest temple, **Senso-ji** in Asakusa (Free). Afterward, walk to **Ueno Park** and shop for budget souvenirs or cheap street food at the bustling **Ameyoko Market**.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 24: Tokyo (teamLab Planets - Rescheduled)**
*   **Activity**: Visit **teamLab Planets** in Toyosu. *(Conditional: You must request a date-change for your ticket from Day 22 to Day 24 through your ticket provider, citing the venue's emergency closure. If a reschedule is unavailable, request a full refund to immediately recover ¥3,800/₹2,100 to your budget, and substitute this morning with a free walk around the Tsukiji Outer Market).*
*   **Afternoon**: Explore **Odaiba** waterfront. Take photos of the Rainbow Bridge and the Giant Unicorn Gundam Statue (Free).
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 25: Tokyo (Akihabara & Ginza Street Photography)**
*   **Activity**: Walk through **Akihabara** for electronics, anime, and gaming culture. In the afternoon, head to **Ginza** for street photography (especially beautiful on weekends when the main street becomes a pedestrian-only zone).
*   **Dining**: Eat budget yakitori at the atmospheric under-the-tracks alleys of Yurakucho (Yurakucho Guard).
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 26: Tokyo (Shibuya & Yoyogi Park)**
*   **Activity**: Photograph the famous **Shibuya Crossing** and the Hachiko Statue. Spend a relaxing afternoon in **Yoyogi Park**, a great spot for people-watching and budget picnics.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 27: Tokyo (Final Prep & Hidden Gems)**
*   **Activity**: Revisit your favorite thrift spots in Shimokitazawa or Koenji for final purchases, or do a street photography sweep of Yanaka Ginza (a charming, old-school Tokyo neighborhood). Pack and prepare for your departure tomorrow.
*   **Accommodation**: Tokyo Hostel.

#### **Day 28: Tokyo ➔ Narita Airport (NRT) ➔ Departure**
*   **Morning**: Check out of the hostel. 
*   **Transport**: Take the **Keisei Access Express** or **Keisei Main Line** from Nippori or Ueno Station directly to Narita Airport. At approx. ¥1,270–¥1,400, this is less than half the price of the Narita Express (N'EX).
*   **Departure**: Board your locked, non-changeable flight home.
